About Us

The Gobobo Group, founded in 2012, is a diversified company with three prominent subsidiaries: Gobobo Projects, Gobobo Waste and Gobobo Energy. Under the leadership of Phillip G. Mahlangu, the company upholds the value of professionalism and has achieved success in various sectors.

Gobobo Projects, one of the subsidiaries of the Gobobo Group, has been providing exceptional services since its establishment. The company has served numerous business organizations, taxi operations, and local government entities. Gobobo Projects takes particular pride in its commitment to serving disadvantaged residents in both Tshwane and the City of Johannesburg. Thanks to Phillip G. Mahlangu's astute business acumen, the company continues to excel in its operations.

Another notable subsidiary of the Gobobo Group is Gobobo Energy, a petroleum wholesaler based in Johannesburg. This 100% black-owned and managed company has established itself as a reputable player in the industry. Gobobo Energy proudly serves esteemed clients such as Denel Aviation, Bophelong Construction, Mapitsi, Motsamai Waste, and Elangeni Cash and Carry. The core business of Gobobo Energy includes sales, marketing, supply, and distribution of various petroleum products, including petrol, diesel fuel, JetA1 fuel, AV Gas, and lubricants.

In addition to Gobobo Projects and Gobobo Energy, the Gobobo Group also encompasses Gobobo Waste, a subsidiary focused on waste management. Under the umbrella of the Gobobo Group, Gobobo Waste is dedicated to providing efficient and sustainable waste management solutions in South Africa. With a strong commitment to environmental responsibility, Gobobo Waste offers comprehensive waste management services to a diverse range of clients.

Furthermore, the Gobobo Group has expanded its portfolio to include Gobobo Farming and Gobobo Minerals. Gobobo Farming operates in the town of Heidelberg and specializes in cattle farming, with plans to expand into pig and sheep farming and establish its own butchery.

Gobobo Minerals is involved in the mining and distribution of coal. With a partnership with Transnet trains, Gobobo Minerals transports and distributes coal to various destinations, contributing to the country's energy sector.

Gobobo Group - Combined Master Profile

Welcome to the consolidated enterprise overview of the Gobobo Group. Established in 2012 under the leadership of Phillip G. Mahlangu, our group unifies five key industries under one uncompromising standard of excellence.

1. Gobobo Projects

Specializes in heavy transport, logistics, storm water pipe installation, plant hire, general construction, and civil road works supporting local governments and municipal entities.

2. Gobobo Energy

A premier 100% black-owned petroleum wholesaler providing bulk distribution of diesel, petrol, illuminating paraffin, Jet A1 aviation fuel, and specialized lubricants for commercial, construction, and aviation clients.

3. Gobobo Waste

Dedicated environmental sanitation partners providing illegal dumping site cleanup, informal settlement waste management, mini disposal site maintenance, and collaborative municipal contracts with Pikitup.

4. Gobobo Farming & Minerals

Operating livestock facilities in Heidelberg focused on cattle rearing with upcoming butchery integration, alongside strategic railway logistics for coal distribution.

5. Minerals

Operating strategic railway logistics for coal distribution.
